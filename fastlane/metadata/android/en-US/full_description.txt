Pocket Foundry runs the official Linux (arm64) build of Factorio on your phone, without emulation, drawn by the phone's GPU. It's an unofficial launcher: you need your own copy of Factorio from factorio.com, and the app holds no part of the game.

<b>How it works</b>
<ul>
<li>Import the Linux (arm64) .tar.xz from factorio.com/download once. The game stays in the app's storage and is never modified.</li>
<li>A small Linux runtime, built from source with the app (glibc, Mesa, SDL, libhybris and Termux:X11's X server), runs it.</li>
<li>On-screen controls made for Factorio's controller mode, a trackpad mode, or a real controller, keyboard and mouse.</li>
<li>Saves, mods and config show up in the phone's Files app.</li>
</ul>

<b>Graphics drivers</b>
Pocket Foundry can draw through the phone's own graphics driver. On first start it offers to download a better one for your GPU, such as Turnip for Adreno 6xx and newer, from github.com/JulioFerrero/pocket-foundry-drivers, where they are built from source. Those downloads come from outside F-Droid and aren't checked by it. You can decline and play on the phone's own driver, which works on fewer phones.

Needs Android 10 or later on an arm64 phone. Phones tested so far: https://pocketfoundry.dev/phones/
